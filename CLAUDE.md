# Writing style rules

- No em dashes (—) in copy or content. Use commas, colons, or restructure the sentence.
- No AI slop: avoid filler phrases like "delve into", "leverage", "game-changer", "seamlessly", "robust", "cutting-edge", "transformative", "it's worth noting", "in conclusion".
- Write like a real person, not a resume bot.
